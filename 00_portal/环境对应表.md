下表环境目录均相对于 `/root/linux_python_offline_resources/`。

| 学习内容 | Linux x86-64 环境目录 | 板端/MCU |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NumPy/SciPy/绘图、P01/P07/P19 | `environments/01_scientific_notebook_py310` 与 `_py312` | 不可用 x86 wheel |
| 统计时序、P06 | `02_ml_stats_timeseries_em_*`、`13_stats_timeseries_*` | — |

| 深度学习、P09 进阶 | `04_pytorch_cpu_*`、`07_tensorflow_cpu_*` | Jetson 用 L4T，勿混 |
| 优化与控制、P08/P05 | `11_optimization_control_*` | — |
| RL、P20 进阶 | `08_rl_basics_*` | — |
| NLP | `09_huggingface_llm_*`、`15_nlp_retrieval_*` | 权重大，本知识库未下模型 |
